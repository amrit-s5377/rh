The Property

About the Roosevelt

The Roosevelt Hotel is a Trademark Collection by Wyndham property in the heart of Williston, North Dakota — pairing the warmth of American heritage with the comforts of contemporary travel. Located at 1001 24th Street West, just off US-2, it serves both business travelers in the Bakken energy corridor and leisure guests exploring western North Dakota.

Every guest room includes a flat-screen HDTV, work desk, coffee/tea maker, microwave, and mini-fridge, with complimentary high-speed WiFi throughout. Guests enjoy a complimentary hot breakfast each morning, and our meeting space hosts corporate events of all sizes.

The Roosevelt Villa

A residential-style suite with room to live, work, and settle in — made for longer stays in Williston.

Extended Stay

A Place to Settle In

The Roosevelt Villa is our most spacious accommodation — a residential-style suite that pairs a separate living area and kitchenette with a comfortable sleeping space. It's built for the guest who needs more than a single night: relocating professionals, visiting families, and crews working the Williston Basin who want the comfort of home with the service of a hotel.

The Space

Living & Kitchen

Stretch out in a separate living area with a comfortable seating zone and dining nook, then keep your routine going in a kitchenette stocked for everyday meals.

  • Separate living & dining area
  • Kitchenette with microwave & refrigerator
  • Coffee/tea maker & everyday tableware
  • Flat-screen HDTV & complimentary high-speed WiFi
Rest

Sleeping Comfort

A quiet, non-smoking sleeping area with premium bedding sits apart from the living space, so you can wind down without the day spilling over into the night.

  • Plush queen bedding, non-smoking
  • Blackout window treatments
  • Generous closet & storage
  • Private bathroom with fresh linens
Made for the Long Haul

Built for Long Stays

Everything you need to keep working and living comfortably for a week, a month, or a season — without the friction of a short-term rental.

  • Dedicated work desk & reliable WiFi
  • Complimentary hot breakfast each morning
  • On-site fitness center & free parking
  • Front desk staffed around the clock

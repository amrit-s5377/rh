Start every morning with a complimentary hot breakfast buffet — scrambled eggs, breakfast meats, and a make-your-own waffle station alongside fresh pastries, fruit, and yogurt. Brew a cup from the coffee and juice station, sit down for a proper meal, or grab something to go before an early start.

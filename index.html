Our Story

A Landmark in Williston

The Roosevelt Hotel is a welcoming retreat in the heart of Williston, North Dakota — a Trademark Collection by Wyndham property that pairs the warmth of American heritage with the comforts of contemporary travel. From the moment you arrive, you're cared for like family.

Situated at 1001 24th Street West, just off US-2, the hotel puts you minutes from the Williston Basin energy corridor and a short drive from Lake Sakakawea, the Missouri River, and the rugged badlands of Theodore Roosevelt National Park. Whether you're here for work in the Bakken or exploring western North Dakota, the Roosevelt is your home away from home.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Is breakfast included with every stay?

Yes. A complimentary hot breakfast buffet is served every morning — scrambled eggs, a make-your-own waffle station, breakfast meats, fresh pastries, fruit, yogurt, and coffee, tea & juice. It's included with your room at no extra charge.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Our front desk is staffed 24 hours a day, so late or early arrivals are no problem. Early check-in and late check-out may be available on request.

Is parking free?

Yes — complimentary on-site parking is available right out front, with plenty of space and easy in-and-out for every guest. On-site EV charging stations are also available.

Is the Roosevelt Hotel pet-friendly?

Pets are welcome in select rooms; a fee may apply. Please let us know when you book so we can set aside the right room for you and your companion.

Do you have rooms with accessibility features?

Yes. We offer mobility-accessible queen rooms with roll-in showers, in both single-queen and two-queen-bed layouts. All of our rooms are non-smoking.

Where is the hotel located?

We're at 1001 24th Street West, Williston, ND 58801 — just off US-2, minutes from the Bakken energy corridor and about 11 miles from Williston Basin International Airport (XWA).

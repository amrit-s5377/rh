Welcome to Williston, ND
Our Story

A Landmark in Williston

The Roosevelt Hotel is a welcoming retreat in the heart of Williston, North Dakota — a Trademark Collection by Wyndham property that pairs the warmth of American heritage with the comforts of contemporary travel. From the moment you arrive, you're cared for like family.

Situated at 1001 24th Street West, just off US-2, the hotel puts you minutes from the Williston Basin energy corridor and a short drive from Lake Sakakawea, the Missouri River, and the rugged badlands of Theodore Roosevelt National Park. Whether you're here for work in the Bakken or exploring western North Dakota, the Roosevelt is your home away from home.
